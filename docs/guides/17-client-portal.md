# 17 · Client portal

**Who / where:** your client's facility manager · same website, client login → lands on the portal

▶ Video: [17-client-portal.webm](videos/17-client-portal.webm) (33 s)

## What it's for
Your clients (malls, tech parks, housing societies) see the service they're paying for: guards on duty, monthly
attendance, invoices, statement and incidents. They see only their own sites, with no pay data.

## Before you start
* **Settings & users → Add user**, role **Client admin** (can report incidents, and confirm / dispute attendance if sign-off is on) or
  **Client viewer** (read only), linked to the client.

## Steps
1. **Today:** per site, guards required vs reported on duty (FILLED / PARTIAL), the outstanding amount and open
   incidents. Pick another date to look back.

   ![Today](img/17-today.png)
2. **Attendance:** month and site → required vs reported per day and who worked which day. Client admins
   **Confirm month** or **Dispute**, only if your agency switched client sign-off on (guide 11; off by default).

   ![Attendance](img/17-attendance.png)
3. **Invoices:** issued invoices only, with period, due date, total and balance. Click one to view or print it.

   ![Invoices](img/17-invoices.png)
4. **Statement:** invoices, credit notes and payments with a running balance.

   ![Statement](img/17-statement.png)
5. **Incidents:** incidents you've shared with them and the ones they reported; client admins can **Report incident**.

## What the system does for you
* Clients can never see other clients, draft invoices, guard pay or internal incidents.
* Disputes reach the agency immediately and hold that month's invoice until resolved.

## Common situations
* **Client can't sign off.** They have a Client viewer login; change it to Client admin.
* **Client sees "PARTIAL" today.** Fewer guards have checked in than required, possibly because the night shift hasn't started yet.
* **Invoice missing in the portal.** Only issued invoices are shown.
